# Plot 20 hybrid architectural environment

The exterior and all 13 apartment outlooks share one outdoor scene in the existing Three.js renderer. The registered origin is 17.402192529° N, 78.312086932° E inside the 16-vertex HMDA Plot 20 boundary. The terrain, public road centerlines and surrounding footprints use that same local metre coordinate frame. The tower placement, finished floor elevation, road widths, planting and surface treatments retain their documented conceptual status. [Geographic coverage](GEOGRAPHIC_COVERAGE_REPORT.md) · [Apartment placement](SPATIAL_ALIGNMENT.md)

## Ground and architecture

The active ground is one triangulated surface with a near DEM and an adjoining regional DEM. Each shared boundary vertex uses the same height. Soil and grass textures use world coordinates. A shader measures distance from the actual parcel edges, applies the near-property material, and blends toward authorized Esri imagery from 8 to 32 metres beyond the boundary. The two distances are exposed together in the hybrid terrain module. The near surface, mapped roads, entrance drives, retained masterplan landscaping and added perimeter specimens remain available when imagery is denied or absent. The fallback uses textured ground throughout. [Implementation](../dist/hybrid-landscape.js)

The existing OSM road pipeline supplies connected centerlines with terrain-conforming polygon surfaces. Road widths, medians, lighting, landscape finishes and entrance links include modeled estimates. The road network retains its published source data. One unsurveyed 4.5 m building extrusion at the entrance has been omitted because its footprint intersects the estimated road surface. The original footprint data remains in the source package and the runtime reports the exclusion. [Road source and geometry audit](NEIGHBORHOOD_REBUILD.md) · [Building sources](BUILDING_DATA_SOURCES.md)

Surrounding buildings use recorded footprints with muted walls and slate or terracotta roof colors. Most heights are class estimates. Their façades are approximate. Trees added by this integration are limited to the parcel, and existing terrace gardens remain in the architecture. The proposed site's detailed materials and furnishing remain illustrative.

## Esri access and arrival

The supported ArcGIS raster endpoint, origin-restricted browser-token authentication, source citation and cache checks, tile refinement, resident tile cap, mip filtering, crossfade and attribution are wired into the active terrain material. A second, coarser tile working set supports the regional surface. Tile bytes are transient. The local near terrain and regional terrain each have up to 64 resident tiles on desktop and 32 on mobile. Regional requests are selected from elevated views. Static shadows update for scene geometry or lighting changes, while image tile arrivals request a color redraw.

Imagery requests have a 12-second deadline. Temporary failures retry automatically after 1.5, 5 and 15 seconds, including when the camera is stationary. After these attempts, the footer offers **Retry map**. Authorization failures wait for a manual retry. Loading and reconnecting have separate messages so pending imagery is not described as unavailable. Pausing or disposing the scene cancels retry timers and pending requests.

When both near and regional licensed imagery are available, the opening sequence follows the regional approach before settling into the building hero. Configured services receive a bounded preload opportunity. The architectural opening remains available during service failures. The user configured an existing Esri browser key during this task. Live imagery delivery then succeeded in Chrome. The endpoint rejected the previous Authorization header during CORS preflight, so the client now uses Esri’s supported token parameter. Public citation metadata uses the public citation endpoint. Synthetic fixtures additionally exercise service denial and shader sampling. Billing configuration was untouched. [Configuration and service terms](ESRI_INTEGRATION.md)

The terrain combines the independently licensed SRTM/Mapzen EGM96 package with live Esri World Elevation samples from Airbus WorldDEM4Ortho. A bounded 65 × 65 sample grid covers the central ±2,048 m at 64 m spacing, using the 24.74 m native source. The app subtracts the Esri origin sample to establish a relative local elevation, retains the conceptual pad, and blends toward the outer open DEM between 1,536 and 2,048 m. This is an approximate local vertical registration between EGM2008 and EGM96, with a surveyed finished-floor reference still required. All samples must pass source, resolution, coverage and datum validation before the scene is built. A partial response retains the open DEM. No verified textured local 3D scene layer was found. The reported 0.31 m imagery native resolution does not guarantee survey-level placement, and the published image accuracy is 8.47 m. [Coverage findings](GEOGRAPHIC_COVERAGE_REPORT.md)

## Visual and architectural limits

The live browser now shows licensed satellite imagery beyond the authored ground. The surrounding building façades remain schematic and the full scene still falls short of the brochure’s photorealistic rendering. Close-range vegetation, furniture and the repeated exterior façade remain approximations. The existing spatial alignment report describes the unverified correspondence between interior and exterior openings. Physical mobile devices remain outside the available test setup.
