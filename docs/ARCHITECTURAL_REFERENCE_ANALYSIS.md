# Brigade Barcelona architectural reference analysis

The bundled 26-page [Brigade Group brochure](../sources/brigade-barcelona-brochure.pdf) matches the copy found in Downloads byte for byte. The six screenshots supplied in [building](../building/) were inspected at original resolution. They depict the same brochure artwork with alternate crops. Five files are 1920 × 1080 screenshots with viewer chrome. The façade crop is 400 × 787. The [reference manifest](../dist/data/architectural-reference-manifest.json) records the source files, dimensions and evidence pages.

Page 3 calls the project **52 storeys** and describes a **seven-level clubhouse**. Page 15 documents floors **17–20** and **22–26** and the **floor 12 refuge plate**. The existing local-metre scene registration ties the brochure plan outlines to HMDA Plot 20. Use it for footprints, amenity positions and selection. Compare apparent façade proportions between rectified crops or corresponding elevations. Rendered perspectives supply relative design intent and cannot establish new site dimensions or saleable floors.

## Existing exterior audit

| Component | Baseline evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Geometry and scale | The detailed and coarse GLBs share the same envelope. The measured asset bounds are about 160 m for Wing A and 178 m for Wing B, including the conceptual stepped roofs. The [scene map](../dist/assets/scene-map.json) retains wing, floor and apartment positions. These are model bounds. |
| Façade | [Generator](../scripts/generate_exterior.py) creates projecting slabs, piers, glazing proxies and balconies from trace rectangles. Much of each bay is a single dark teal glass volume behind a regular grid. The brochure shows a stronger distinction between narrow punched terracotta openings and the broad ivory balcony bays. |
| Crowns | Each bay has a flat-topped cornice stack and repeating columns. The brochure’s upper panels have a deeper top shadow line and a broad, legible Brigade nameplate. |
| Podium and clubhouse | Both use actual geometry. The lateral clubhouse view is dominated by straight, nearly identical bands and vertical glazing. Page 12 shows longer slopes, stronger overhangs and recessed glazing. The podium screens and landscape appear sparse at street level. |
| Landscaping | Low-poly plant meshes use GPU instances and color variation. Repeated round canopies and low planted edges read as visibly sparse next to the artwork. |
| Rendering | Three.js uses ACES filmic tone mapping, an HDR environment, directional solar presets, shadows and adaptive pixel ratio. Dark teal panes plus bright flat finishes give the elevation a high-contrast grid. The geographic context uses its existing real terrain, imagery and footprint layers. |
| Performance | The [prior isolated model benchmark](../qa/exterior-geometry.json) recorded about 505,000 triangles for the detailed model and 101,000 for the distant model. The [new browser baseline](../qa/evidence/architectural-baseline/capture.json) also includes geographic context. Triangle and draw-call counts vary with camera position and geographic streaming. |

The [baseline captures](../qa/evidence/architectural-baseline/) record front, rear, left, right, aerial, street-level, façade, rooftop, clubhouse and podium views before asset replacement. The baseline asset hashes are in [the hash list](../qa/evidence/architectural-baseline/asset-sha256.txt).
