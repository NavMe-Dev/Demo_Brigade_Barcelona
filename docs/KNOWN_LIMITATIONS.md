# Architectural and testing limits

- **Height and floors.** The supplied brief and secondary sales reference say 53 storeys. Page 3 of the 26-page developer brochure says 52. The model follows the brochure’s stepped silhouette. Page 15 gives design assignments only for floor 12, floors 17–20 and floors 22–26. The A7 location on floor 12 serves as a refuge. Other levels retain their indicative exterior geometry and await a confirmed plan.
- **Site registration.** HMDA supplies the parcel vertices. The tower’s position inside the plot comes from a proportional registration of the developer’s masterplan, with a four-corner RMS residual of 1.033 m. Floor heights, external bays, equipment sizes, landscaping, deck levels and façade details are indicative.
- **Apartments.** The 13 designs represent brochure layouts. Measured CAD, window schedules, finished ceiling heights and construction drawings were unavailable. Wall and portal placements, room camera clearances, materials and furniture are interpretations. Only legible published room dimensions are labelled as such. The detailed source plan remains available for each residence.
- **Clubhouse.** The brochure establishes a seven-level clubhouse, a rooftop pool and 16 programme entries. Individual interior floor assignments are unpublished. The interactive programme uses labelled conceptual room studies.
- **Lighting and views.** Solar positions use an approximate equinox simulation at the project’s geographic coordinates. The offline Sentinel layer records March 2025 land cover at 10 m per pixel. The May 2026 Esri source at Plot 20 reports 0.31 m source imagery and 8.47 m positional accuracy. Both record shadows from their acquisition dates. The skyline, neighboring heights, glass reflections and balcony views are simulated. Outdoor and indoor presets illustrate ambience.
- **Devices.** Desktop browsers rendered and exercised actual WebGL geometry. Phone and tablet viewports and touch gestures were emulated on macOS. Physical mobile GPUs, thermal throttling, low-memory devices and sustained battery use require device testing. Published timing and FPS measurements apply to the recorded network and graphics profiles.
- **Reference material.** The supplied screen recording guided the exterior-to-interior transition. The ConnectLabs reference URL was unavailable during review. Developer artwork remains attributed in the local experience. The rights, material specifications and architectural approvals for public commercial distribution require review before publication.

- **Neighborhood coverage.** Of 23,520 mapped footprints, 25 carry a source height, 102 use a recorded floor count and 23,393 use a class estimate. Source height tags have variable provenance. Construction masks, confidence filtering and regional imagery all reflect dated snapshots. No textured local mesh or verified apartments’ balcony sightline was established.
